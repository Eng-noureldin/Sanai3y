## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, boutique, plant-shop-inspired digital marketplace connecting Egyptian homeowners with skilled tradespeople ('صنايعية'). Rather than defaulting to cold, transactional utility or harsh industrial cues common in maintenance platforms, the aesthetic conveys tranquility, professional respect, organic craft, and domestic calm.

### Core Character & Mood
- **Organic Reliability:** Natural hues, botanical greenery, and cream paper-like textures inspire immediate trust and domestic serenity.
- **Craftsmanship & Dignity:** Elevated typography and delicate linework reframe home trades into dependable craftsmanship.
- **RTL-Native Ergonomics:** Built deliberately for Arabic-first reading flow, prioritizing natural eye scans from right to left with custom badge alignments, reversed directional controls, and carefully weighted Arabic letterforms.

### Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Boutique Organic Minimalism** with **Tactile Editorial Clarity**: pure white cards elevated over soft linen-cream surfaces, framed with whisper-soft borders (#E1E6D8), punctuated by deep evergreen interactive nodes and fresh mint micro-accents.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from lush botanical botanies, greenhouse glass, and unbleached paper stock.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#26382A` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors high-hierarchy headings, solid primary action buttons, active navigation bars, and primary tab indicators.
- **Secondary (`#5E7A5C` - Sage Green):** Applied to secondary interactive strokes, category icon contours, active sub-filters, and secondary buttons.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#7ED3C0` - Soft Mint):** High-impact positive highlights, live availability indicators, active badges, and focus rings.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F3F5EC` - Pale Cream-Green):** The foundation canvas color across all viewport bodies, delivering lower glare than harsh digital white.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Used strictly for elevated cards, persistent sheets, modal windows, and active inputs.
- **Highlight Strip (`#E3EAD9`):** Accentuated banner backdrops, promotional micro-banners, and secondary structural cards.
- **Text & Stroke System:**
  - Headings & Primary Titles: `#1F2A22`
  - Body & Descriptive Text: `#6B7568`
  - Muted Details & Captions: `#8A9C86`
  - Subtle Borders & Separators: `#E1E6D8`

### Status Token Roles (Arabic Context)
- **Open (`مفتوح`):** Background `#E6F7F3`, Foreground `#227B67`
- **Agreed (`تم الاتفاق`):** Background `#ECEAF8`, Foreground `#5A519E`
- **Completed (`مكتمل`):** Background `#E8F4E8`, Foreground `#2E6B30`
- **Pending (`قيد الانتظار`):** Background `#FEF8E7`, Foreground `#8F6B12`
- **Cancelled (`ملغي`):** Background `#EFEFEF`, Foreground `#666666`

## Typography

The typographic stack prioritizes native Arabic legibility via **Cairo** for structural titles and prominent CTAs, complemented by **Tajawal** for fluid body and descriptive reading. Latin numeral and secondary fallbacks fall to Plus Jakarta Sans.

### RTL Dynamics & Line-Height Buffer
Arabic glyphs contain taller ascenders and deeper descenders than standard Latin typography. All line-height values include an extended 25–35% vertical buffer to prevent vowel mark (harakat) clipping and maintain rhythmic breathing space.

### Currency Formatting
Egyptian Pound (`ج.م.`) follows eastern Arabic numbering or clean international digits aligned logically: e.g., `٣٥٠ ج.م.` or `350 ج.م.` set in `label-lg` or `headline-md` weights, ensuring the currency symbol is placed consistently to the left of the numeral in RTL reading order.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid for desktop views (max-width: 1240px) scaling down to a single-column stacked layout on mobile devices.

### Fluid Grid Adaptations
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) page side padding.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) page side padding.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns / single vertical flow, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) horizontal page margins.

### Directional Spacing Strategy
All left/right padding and margins are mapped dynamically to logical layout boundaries:
- Inward icon buffers use `margin-inline-start` / `margin-inline-end`.
- Interactive chevron indicators point leftward (`←` / `chevron-left`) to signify forward navigation in RTL screens.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the tactile boutique identity, this design system avoids heavy, murky drop-shadows. Depth is established through **tonal layering** and **soft ambient illumination**.

### Layering Model
1. **Base Plane (Floor):** Canvas background `#F3F5EC` provides an earthy, warm foundation.
2. **Surface Plane:** Interactive container cards use pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by hairline `#E1E6D8` borders (1px solid).
3. **Ambient Lift (Cards on Hover / Active Drawers):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(38, 56, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(38, 56, 42, 0.03);`
4. **Floating Overlays (Modals & Bottom Navigation):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(38, 56, 42, 0.10); border: 1px solid #E1E6D8;`

The subtle green-tinted shadow (`rgba(38, 56, 42, *)`) keeps illumination visually cohesive with the deep forest green primary identity.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a consistent **8px (`0.5rem`) border radius** across interactive containers, cards, inputs, and standard buttons. This curvature mirrors soft river stones and natural architectural forms without devolving into childish bubble geometry.

### Radius Assignments
- **Core Elements (Cards, Text Inputs, Base Buttons):** `8px` (`0.5rem`)
- **Pill Badges (Verification 'موثّق', Category Chips, Status Tags):** Fully rounded pill format (`9999px`)
- **Nested Inner Assets (Trade category thumbnails, avatar containers):** `6px` (`0.375rem`) to maintain proportional concentricity within parent `8px` frames.

## Components

- **Primary Button:** Background `#26382A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `8px`, height `48px`, font weight `700`. Hover state triggers background shift to `#314636`. Active state shifts to `#1F2A22`.
- **Secondary Button:** Background `transparent`, border `1.5px solid #5E7A5C`, text `#5E7A5C`, border-radius `8px`. Hover state introduces `#F3F5EC` fill.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Background `transparent`, text `#26382A`, underline on hover.

### Verified Artisan Badge ('موثّق')
- High-trust emblem pill: background `#E6F7F3`, border `1px solid #7ED3C0`, text `#227B67`, typography `label-sm`.
- Features a small green checkmark icon positioned at the right (inline-start) of the Arabic label.

### Trade Category Cards (Thin Sage Outline)
- Aspect ratio `1:1` on mobile carousels; card padding `16px`.
- Border `1px solid #E1E6D8`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Iconography: Monoline `1.5px` trade tools (plumbing, carpentry, electrical) drawn exclusively in sage green (`#5E7A5C`).
- Active / Selected state: Border shifts to `2px solid #26382A`, background reveals a subtle `#F3F5EC` tint.
